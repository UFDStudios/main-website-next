Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 13-June-2025

Welcome to UFD Studios ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile games and services ("Games" or "Services").

We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and complying with international data protection laws, including:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) – For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) – For California residents

By using our Games, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect both personal and non-personal data to operate and improve our Services.

1.1 Personal Data (Provided by You)

  • Name and contact information (e.g., email address if submitted via support)
  • Payment data (processed securely by Google Play, Apple, etc. — we do not store payment details)
  • User-generated content (e.g., chat messages, profile details)

Note: Some features may not work if personal data is not provided.

1.2 Non-Personal Data (Collected Automatically)

  • Device data: OS, model, IP address, advertising ID
  • Gameplay & usage data: Session duration, in-game events, device interactions
  • Location data (if permission is granted)

1.3 Third-Party Data

  • Social Logins: If you log in with a third-party service (e.g., Facebook or Google), we may receive data such as your name, profile photo, and email address.
  • Ad & Analytics Providers: We receive aggregate data regarding user behavior and ad performance.

1.4 Device Permissions

Our games may request access to:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Storage

You can manage permissions via:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → [Game] → Permissions
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → [Permission Type] → [Game]

Denying permissions may limit certain features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Enhance game functionality and user experience
  • Analyze and improve performance
  • Deliver personalized and non-personalized ads
  • Detect fraud and prevent abuse

Consent for Ads: In some regions (like the EU), we will request explicit consent before collecting data for ad targeting.
